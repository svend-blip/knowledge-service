Reading: the two providers agree on roughly a third of their top-5 (0.11–0.67 per query) and rank differently; neither list is obviously wrong. On this host the portable provider answers ~160× faster per query because LEANN recomputes passage embeddings on every search in daemon-free mode. The portable provider is a credible default for GPU-less machines; a quality verdict needs the same-task evaluation, not this overlap number.
